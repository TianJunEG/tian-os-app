HOW TO GET SCIENCE HOD EMAIL ADDRESSES
========================================

METHOD 1: DIRECT PHONE CONTACT
- Call the school's main phone number
- Ask for: "Science Department" or "Head of Department Science"
- Request their email address

METHOD 2: EMAIL INQUIRY
- Send email to school's main address
- Subject: "Request for Science HOD Contact Information"
- Schools will forward to appropriate HOD

METHOD 3: WEBSITE STAFF DIRECTORY
- Visit school website
- Look for: "Our People", "Staff Directory", "School Executive Committee", or "Department Leaders"
- Some schools list HOD names (and occasionally emails)

IMPORTANT NOTE ON DATA AVAILABILITY
====================================

Singapore schools DO NOT publicly list individual staff email addresses for privacy protection.

WHAT IS PUBLIC:
- School main email address
- School phone number
- HOD names (sometimes)
- Department information

WHAT REQUIRES DIRECT CONTACT:
- Individual HOD email addresses
- Direct HOD contact details
- Specific departmental inquiries
