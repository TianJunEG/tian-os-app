Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Tutor Match

How much can I earn?

Teachers typically earn $35-60 per hour depending on subject expertise, experience, and student demand. The platform handles payments securely.

Do I need to have a teaching qualification?

While not required, relevant qualifications or experience are important. We look for educators with subject expertise and the ability to help students succeed.

Can I teach online and in-person?

Yes! Our platform supports both online and in-person lessons. You decide which works best for you.

What's the approval process?

After signup, our team reviews your profile within 24-48 hours. We may ask for verification of qualifications and conduct a brief video call to ensure fit.

Can I use the PSLE exam prep software?

Absolutely! All Tutor Match educators get access to our science exam prep software to enhance your lessons and help students prepare for PSLE.

How do I get support?

You'll have access to our teacher support team via email and chat. We're here to help you succeed on the platform.
