HOW TO USE:
===========

1. BULK EMAIL OUTREACH:
   - Open CSV in Excel or Google Sheets
   - Extract email column (Column 6: Email Address)
   - Import to your email marketing platform
   - All schools use format: school_name@moe.edu.sg

2. PHONE OUTREACH:
   - Use phone numbers in Column 7
   - Call during office hours (typically 8am-5pm)
   - Ask for school office or HOD Science

3. GEOGRAPHIC TARGETING:
   - Filter by Zone column to target specific regions
   - North cluster for northern suburbs campaigns
   - East cluster for eastern zone expansion
   - West cluster for western expansion

4. SCHOOL TYPE SEGMENTATION:
   - Government schools for standard curriculum
   - Madrasahs for Islamic curriculum integration
   - Special ed schools for inclusive programs

5. WEBSITE OUTREACH:
   - Use website URLs (Column 8) to research schools
   - Check school programs before outreach
   - Identify existing science/STEM initiatives

NOTES FOR OUTREACH:
===================

Email Best Practices:
• Keep email professional and brief
• Reference specific school programs if available
• Include clear call-to-action
• Provide trial period or demo opportunity
• Respect email policies - some schools may have restrictions

Phone Outreach:
• Identify yourself and your organization clearly
• Ask for the right contact (usually HOD Science)
• Have specific program info ready
• Follow up with email if they request
• Note: School hours are typically 8am-5pm, Monday-Friday

School Liaison:
• Coordinate with HOD Science for subject-specific programs
• Follow up with admin for procurement processes
• Some schools may need principal approval
• Build relationships with key contacts

SCIENCE HOD INFORMATION:
========================
Science HOD names are typically NOT publicly listed on school websites.
To find them:
1. Call the school main line and ask for HOD Science department
2. Visit the school in person and ask at the office
3. Check school annual reports (if available)
4. Request intro from existing school contacts
