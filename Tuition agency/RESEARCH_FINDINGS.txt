CRITICAL FINDING: PRIVACY PROTECTION FOR INDIVIDUAL STAFF
===========================================================

Singapore schools, particularly MOE government-aided schools, DO NOT publicly list individual staff member email addresses on their websites. This is a deliberate privacy protection measure.

What IS publicly available:
- School main contact email (e.g., fhps@moe.edu.sg)
- School phone number
- HOD names (on some schools' staff directories)
- Department information

What is NOT publicly available:
- Individual HOD email addresses
- Individual teacher email addresses

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR OBTAINING SCIENCE HOD CONTACT INFORMATION
=============================================================

1. DIRECT CONTACT METHOD:
   - Call the school's main office number
   - Ask to be transferred to the Science Department or the Science HOD
   - Schools maintain internal staff directories with email addresses

2. EMAIL METHOD:
   - Use the school's main email address
   - Subject: "Contact Information for Science Head of Department"
   - Schools will forward your inquiry to the appropriate HOD

3. SCHOOL WEBSITES:
   - Most MOE schools have staff directory pages (e.g., "Our People", "School Executive Committee", "Departments")
   - Look for pages like: Staff Directory, Department Leaders, Academic Departments
   - Some schools list HOD names but not emails
