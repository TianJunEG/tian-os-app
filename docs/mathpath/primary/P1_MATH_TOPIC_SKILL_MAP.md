# Primary 1 Math Topic Skill Map

Status: curriculum planning reference  
Scope: Tian OS MathPath Primary 1  
Runtime impact: none

## Purpose

This document maps Primary 1 mathematics into teachable Tian OS micro-skills for future diagnostic, practice, fluency, remediation, worksheet generation, and parent/tutor support.

This is a docs-only curriculum planning file. It does not modify runtime code, seed data, question generators, routes, dashboards, or tests.

## Design principles

- Keep Primary 1 skills concrete and visual before symbolic.
- Separate concept understanding from fluency.
- Track common early misconceptions explicitly.
- Use original Tian OS question contexts and examples only.
- Avoid assuming working evidence is needed for every Primary 1 item; many tasks rely on visual selection, counting, or oral/mental reasoning.
- Use parent/tutor notes to explain what adults should observe, not just whether the answer is right.

## Topic groups

1. Numbers to 10 / 20 / 100
2. Addition and Subtraction
3. Equal Groups / Sharing Readiness
4. Money
5. Measurement
6. Geometry
7. Picture Graphs / Data

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| P1-NUM-06 | Order numbers | Arrange numbers from smallest to greatest or greatest to smallest. | P1-NUM-05 | Check sequence and comparison across several numbers. | Put these numbers from smallest to greatest: 12, 8, 15. | Orders in the given order or confuses teen numbers. | Place numbers on a number line before ordering. | Order three new numbers. | Optional number line | Yes | Ask: which comes first when we count? |
| P1-NUM-07 | Before, after, and between | Identify neighbouring numbers in a counting sequence. | P1-NUM-04 | Check sequence flexibility. | What number is between 13 and 15? | Counts from 1 every time or confuses before/after. | Practise counting on and counting back around the target number. | Ask before/after/between with new values. | Optional number line | Strong yes | Important for mental addition/subtraction readiness. |
| P1-NUM-08 | Number bonds within 10 | Split a number into two parts. | P1-NUM-01 | Check part-whole understanding. | Complete: 7 = 5 + ___. | Counts all again or guesses the missing part. | Use counters: show 7, cover 5, count the hidden part. | Use another bond within 10. | Useful | Strong yes | Number bonds are foundational for mental arithmetic. |

| P1-NUM-10 | Tens and ones | Two-digit numbers are made of tens and ones. | Counting to 20/100 | Check place-value understanding. | How many tens and ones are in 34? | Says 34 has 3 ones and 4 tens, or treats digits as separate labels. | Use base-ten blocks or bundles of ten and loose ones. | Ask for tens and ones in another two-digit number. | Required for early learners | Later, after concept is secure | Place value weakness affects addition/subtraction later. |
| P1-NUM-11 | Count in tens and ones | Count grouped tens and leftover ones. | P1-NUM-10 | Check whether child can combine tens and ones into a number. | There are 4 tens and 3 ones. | P1-ADD-04 | Missing number addition | Find the missing part or addend. | P1-NUM-08, P1-ADD-01 | Check inverse/part-whole reasoning. | 6 + ___ = 10. | Adds all visible numbers or treats blank as zero. | Use counters: show total 10, known part 6, find hidden part. | Use a new missing-addend equation. | Useful | Yes after concept | This is a common diagnostic point for deeper understanding. |

| P1-SUB-02 | Subtract within 20 | Subtract using counting back, number line, or bonds. | P1-SUB-01, P1-NUM-07 | Check subtraction beyond 10. | Find 15 - 6. | Counts forward or reverses numbers. | Use number line to count back 6 from 15. | Similar subtraction within 20. | Optional number line | Strong yes | Watch for children who always count from 1. |
| P1-SUB-03 | Missing number subtraction | Find unknown start, removed part, or remainder. | P1-SUB-01 | Check flexible subtraction reasoning. | 12 - ___ = 7. | Treats blank as the answer rather than missing removed amount. | Use a part-whole model: total, removed, left. | Use a new missing subtraction equation. | Useful | Later | This separates memorised subtraction from reasoning. |
| P1-AS-01 | Fact families | Addition and subtraction are connected. | P1-ADD-03, P1-SUB-01 | Check inverse relationship. | If 6 + 4 = 10, what is 10 - 6? | Sees each fact as unrelated. | Use one number bond to make two addition and two subtraction facts. | Give another number bond. | Useful | Yes after concept | Helps reduce memorisation load. |
| P1-WP-ADD-01 | One-step addition word problems | Identify combine/add situations. | P1-ADD-01 | Check language-to-operation mapping. | Ali has 5 stickers. He gets 3 more. | P1-WP-SUB-01 | One-step subtraction word problems | Identify take-away/left situations. | P1-SUB-01 | Check language-to-operation mapping. | Mei has 12 grapes. She gives away 4. | P1-WP-CMP-01 | Compare word problems | Find how many more/fewer. | P1-NUM-05, P1-SUB-01 | Check comparison subtraction. | Ben has 9 marbles. Sara has 6. How many more does Ben have? | Adds both groups or gives the larger number. | Align objects or bars and match pairs to see the extra. | New comparison story. | Required for weak learners | No | This is different from take-away subtraction. |

## Top 10 Primary 1 misconceptions

| # | Misconception | Likely topic area | How Tian OS should detect it | Remediation direction |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counting objects twice or skipping objects | Numbers | Wrong count on object sets, especially scattered arrangements | Use one-to-one pointing/crossing-out strategy. |
| 2 | Treating the last number said as unrelated to total quantity | Numbers | Can recite sequence but cannot answer “how many altogether?” | Emphasise cardinality: last number is the total. |
| 3 | Confusing digit reversal, e.g. 12 and 21 | Numbers/place value | Wrong numeral for teen/two-digit numbers | Use tens-and-ones language and base-ten visuals. |
| 4 | Comparing by first digit or appearance only | Numbers/money/data | Chooses visually larger item or larger-looking digit | Use number line, objects, or value comparison. |
| 5 | Thinking addition always means bigger | Addition/subtraction | Fails missing-number or comparison contexts | Teach part-whole model, not just “put together.” |
| 6 | Thinking subtraction only means taking away | Subtraction/word problems | Fails comparison subtraction | Use matched pairs or comparison bars. |
| 7 | Counting all instead of counting on | Addition fluency | Always starts from 1 for addition within 20 | Practise counting on from the larger number. |
| 8 | Confusing tens and ones | Place value | Reverses tens/ones or adds digits | Use bundles of ten and loose ones. |
| 9 | Confusing coin size with coin value | Money | Chooses larger coin as higher value | Read printed value and compare amounts. |
| 10 | Misreading picture graphs by counting all pictures | Data | Counts entire graph when asked one category | Read category label first; cover irrelevant rows. |

## Recommended first implementation slice

### P1-NUM Foundation Slice

Recommended first runtime slice after this docs map:

```text
P1-NUM Foundation Slice:
Counting, number recognition, compare/order, before/after/between, number bonds within 10, and tens/ones readiness.
```

### Why this should come first

Numbers are the foundation for every later P1 topic. Addition, subtraction, money, measurement, geometry data questions, and word problems all depend on stable counting, number recognition, comparison, and part-whole reasoning.

### Suggested runtime anchors later

These can later become a new Primary 1 domain or stage under MathPath, separate from the existing Fractions runtime anchors.

Suggested first skills:

- P1-NUM-01 Count objects accurately
- P1-NUM-02 Match number to quantity
- P1-NUM-04 Count forward and backward
- P1-NUM-05 Compare numbers
- P1-NUM-06 Order numbers
- P1-NUM-07 Before, after, and between
- P1-NUM-08 Number bonds within 10
- P1-NUM-10 Tens and ones

### Files likely touched later

Do not touch these in this docs task, but likely future implementation files may include:

- a Primary 1 skill graph file
- a Primary 1 question generator file
- diagnostic domain registry entries
- visual/object model renderer support
- number-fluency integration
- parent/tutor dashboard labels
- worksheet generator topic selector

### Tests likely needed later

- P1 skill graph defines stable IDs and labels.
- P1 number generator creates object-counting and numeral questions.
- Visual requirements flag object pictures for counting tasks.
- Misconception detection catches count mismatch, digit reversal, place-value confusion, and count-all strategy.
- Fluency tests cover before/after/between and number bonds.

### Pilot risk level

Low if introduced as docs-only or hidden preview. Medium if exposed to students before question generator quality and visual support are validated.

### Visual / fluency / remediation needs

- Visuals: required for counting, quantity matching, place value, geometry, money, graphs.
- Fluency: suitable for number recognition, before/after/between, number bonds, simple addition/subtraction.
- Remediation: must use concrete objects, number lines, ten-frames, and clear parent/tutor guidance.
