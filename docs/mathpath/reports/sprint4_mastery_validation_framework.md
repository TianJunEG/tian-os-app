# Sprint 4 Mastery Validation Framework

Status: Completed

## Mastery States

- Not Started
- Emerging
- Developing
- Secure
- Mastered
- Retained

## Evidence Used

- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Working evidence
- Confidence calibration
- Fluency as a supporting signal

Timing is explicitly not used alone to determine mastery.

## Retention Schedule

When mastery is reached, the foundation retention cadence is:

- 1 day
- 7 days
- 30 days
- 90 days
