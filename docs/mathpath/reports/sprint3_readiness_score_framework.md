## New readiness components
Readiness is now explained through:
- Knowledge
- Fluency
- Confidence calibration
- Retention
- Working evidence quality
- Overall

## Formula
Overall readiness combines:
- Knowledge: 45%
- Fluency: 20%
- Confidence calibration: 15%
- Retention: 10%
- Working evidence: 10%

If retention evidence is unknown, the engine uses current knowledge evidence as a temporary fallback and marks retention as `Unknown` for display.

## Guardrail
Timing is not used alone to determine mastery. It is one signal alongside correctness, confidence, working evidence, and attempts.

## Student-facing explanation
The diagnostic result page now shows the component breakdown and an explanation of how the score was formed.
