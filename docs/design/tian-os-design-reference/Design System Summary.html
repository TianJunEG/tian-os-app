All cards share a 1px hairline border (#EFEDE6), a soft resting shadow (1px ambient + 24px blur, both navy at 4–6% opacity), and a 20px radius by default. Hero cards step up to 24–28px.

Buttons

Navy ★ Gold · AI ✓ Success ✕ Error Outline

One primary action per screen. Sizes: s 36 · m 48 · l 56px tall. All buttons use 14px radius. Gold is reserved for the single moment the system is surfacing something (AI recommendation, mastery, reward).

Icons

Stroked line icons, 1.75 weight, rounded joins. Lucide-style. 24×24 viewBox, drawn at 16–22px in the product. Two-tone module marks (e.g. Σ, Aa, ⌬) use Fraunces in the brand colours — they're identity, not icons. No emoji in chrome (acceptable in user-generated content). No filled icons unless semantic state requires it (e.g. star filled = bookmarked).

Four tabs, never five. Home, MathPath, Pathway, Profile. Modules launch from Home's "All practice" — never crowd the global nav.
2
Practice screens hide the nav. When the student is mid-question or mid-fluency, chrome disappears. Calm focus.
3
Back, not down. Topic and practice screens use a back arrow + title, not a tab swap. Modal-style.
4
Sticky CTA at 88px from bottom. Above the floating nav, with a soft white-fade gradient that protects the button from list content beneath.

Operating principles

1
One main action per screen. The next step is always obvious. Secondary actions are quiet (ghost or chip-sized).
2
Gold is a signal, not a colour. Reserve it for AI flags, mastery moments, and recommended CTAs. If it's gold, the system is talking to the student.
3
Calm over celebratory. No confetti. No big "WELL DONE!". Mastery is acknowledged with a quiet ring fill, a +N delta, and a knock-on effects card.
4
Numbers are tabular. Every percentage, time, and streak uses tabular-nums so they don't shift between screens.
5
Mastery > level. The system displays "P4 · 54% mastered" — not a school year alone. Progress is granular and visible.
6
Worked example before independent practice. Remediation always goes: worked → guided → independent → fluency. Calm and unhurried.
7
Singapore Math vocabulary. Regroup (not borrow). Heuristic (not trick). Concrete → pictorial → abstract.
8
The dashboard is "today", not "everything". 3–4 tasks max. Module sprawl lives behind the Practice launcher.
